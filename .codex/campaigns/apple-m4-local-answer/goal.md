# Apple M4 Local Answer Goal

Make Apple Silicon useful for a local Mac user by turning the receipt-backed proof lane into prompt-in, intelligible-answer-out behavior.

Start with `apple-m4-cpu-neon` as the reliable local-answer path: real GGUF, real tokenizer, strict loader, multi-token greedy output, valid text, stable receipts, and clear fallback status. Keep `apple-m4-metal` limited to receipt-backed phases until full model inference is proven, keep `apple-m4-mpsgraph` as graph/reference evidence, and do not claim Neural Engine, QK256, hidden fallback, or broad performance.
